Why it matters

Give the experience a world of its own.

The strongest leisure concepts have a clear reason to exist beyond the activity. The story shapes what people expect, how they play, what they remember and why the experience could only belong to that brand.

We build that world around the audience and commercial model. The result may include a proposition, narrative, characters, visual identity, activity design and spatial experience. Every part should support the same customer promise and remain practical to operate.

Process

How we build an experience.

  1. 1

    Find the idea worth entering

    We connect the audience, activity and commercial opportunity to one clear creative proposition.

  2. 2

    Build the world

    We develop the story, identity, characters, rules and visual language around the proposition.

  3. 3

    Design the play

    We map the journey, interactions and spatial moments that turn the world into something people can do.

  4. 4

    Make it repeatable

    We consider operations, content renewal, return visits and rollout while the experience is still being designed.

Questions clients ask

Useful answers before we talk.

What counts as a themed experience?+

It can be an attraction, competitive socialising venue, family entertainment concept, immersive event, mini golf course or another activity shaped by a clear world and story. The theme should influence the experience rather than sit on top of it.

Do you only design mini golf experiences?+

No. Mini golf is one area of specialist experience, but the method applies across interactive leisure, entertainment, attractions and mixed-activity venues.

Can you improve a concept that already exists?+

Yes. We can sharpen the proposition, build a stronger world around the activity, redesign the customer journey or create the content needed to make it more distinctive.

How early should you become involved?+

Early involvement gives the concept more influence over the commercial model, site brief and technical plan. We can also join later to solve a defined creative or experience problem.

Do you take the experience through build?+

We can lead the creative development and work alongside the specialist technical and build teams required by the project. The exact delivery role depends on the experience and procurement structure.

Can the concept be designed for several sites?+

Yes. We consider the fixed world, adaptable content, operational requirements and site variables needed to make the experience recognisable and repeatable.
